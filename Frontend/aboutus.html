About UPSA Internship Connect

UPSA Internship Connect is a digital platform designed to bridge the gap between academic education and professional experience. We connect students of the University of Professional Studies, Accra, with organizations across Ghana that provide internship opportunities.

Our goal is to make internship opportunities accessible, simplify the application process and help students develop the practical skills required in today's competitive job market.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower UPSA students by connecting them with valuable internship opportunities, professional networks and practical work experiences that prepare them for successful careers.

👁

Our Vision

To become a leading internship connection platform in Ghana, creating a strong bridge between universities, students and industry partners.

⭐

Our Core Values

Professionalism, integrity, innovation, collaboration, accessibility and commitment to student development.
